VYRA AI Classroom v24

Main fixes:
- Exact NCERT chapter resolution for supported curriculum.
- Class 10 Light maps to jesc109 and retrieves the whole chapter corpus before lesson planning.
- 15-minute classes are generated as rapid revision of the requested topic/chapter instead of generic prerequisite teaching.
- Longer durations increase coverage and explanation rather than repeating points.
- Speech prompt is conversational and non-template-based.
- Browser speech uses a more natural default rate/pitch, prefers available male/natural voices, removes artificial delays between points, and includes an Android/Chrome speech watchdog.

Important:
- Browser speech quality still depends on the voices installed/available on the device. For production-grade human voice, use a dedicated TTS service later.
- Corpus must be indexed locally.
